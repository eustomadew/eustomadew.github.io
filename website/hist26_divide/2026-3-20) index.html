Biography

*Email, Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn, dblp, ORCID, arXiv

I am currently a Marie Skłodowska-Curie postdoctoral fellow, hosted at the Department of Computer Science, University of Copenhagen, and funded by the European Union (the FairML project and CORDIS). I am also a member of the CopeNLU group led by Prof. Isabelle Augenstein. Prior to that, I obtained my Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), and was very fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Huanhuan Chen. My research interests span the areas of machine learning, ensemble methods, and fairness in machine learning. My CV can be found here.
