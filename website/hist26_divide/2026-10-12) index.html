Biography

*Email, Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn, dblp, ORCID, arXiv

I am a researcher at the Centre for Credible AI, Warsaw University of Technology, also a guest researcher at the AI Section, DIKU, University of Copenhagen. Prior to that, I was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie postdoctoral fellow, hosted at the Department of Computer Science, University of Copenhagen, and funded by the European Union (the FairML project and CORDIS). I obtained my Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), and was fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Huanhuan Chen. My research interests span the areas of machine learning, ensemble methods, algorithmic fairness, and trustworthy artificial intelligence. My CV can be found here.
